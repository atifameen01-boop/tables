LitTable AI User Guide

How LitTable AI Helps Narrative Reviews:

  • Citation Extraction: Automatically detects in-text citations like (Smith et al., 2023) or [1] and links them directly into dedicated reference columns in generated tables.
  • Exact Citation Placement: Recommends specific sentence anchor words and approximate line numbers in your draft after which you should cite each table (e.g., (see Table 1)).
  • Editable Output: Every cell, column header, caption, and title is directly editable in real time.
  • Seamless Word Export: Click "Copy for MS Word" and paste directly into Microsoft Word to keep native Word table formatting!
